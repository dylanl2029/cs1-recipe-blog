Cheeseburger

Description

A cheeseburger is a juicy beef patty with melted cheese on a bun. It is one of my favorite foods.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Cheese
  • Burger Bun
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Lettuce and Tomato

Steps

  1. Shape the ground beef into a patty.
  2. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Cook the burger until it is medium rare.
  4. Add cheese onto the patty and let it melt.
  5. Put the burger into a bun and add desired toppings.

Penne alla Vodka

Description

Penne alla Vodka is a pasta dish with a creamy tomato sauce. It is very cheesy and delicious.

Ingredients

  • Penne Pasta
  • Tomato Paste
  • Heavy Cream
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Parmesan Cheese

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta in water
  2. Melt butter and cook garlic
  3. Add tomato paste and stir
  4. Add heavy cream and mix
  5. Mix in the boiled pasta
  6. Add parmesan cheese and serve

Steak

Description

Steak is a flavorful beef dish that can be cooked in a pan or on a grill. It tastes great with garlic and butter.

Ingredients

  • Steak
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Season the steak with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat a pan with some oil.
  3. Cook the steak on both sides and make a crust
  4. Add butter and garlic in the an for flavor
  5. Check that it reaches 135 degrees then let it rest for a few minutes
  6. Serve on a plate and enjoy
